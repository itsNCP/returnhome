Fruita → Green River, UT
~90 mi · I-70 West · Colorado River canyon country
▼
Colorado National Monument
🏔️ Visible to the south as you leave Fruita — sheer red sandstone cliffs rising 2,000 ft above the Grand Valley. I-70 skirts the base.
Utah State Line PHOTO
~20 mi from Fruita · Pull-off at the welcome sign
⏰ No time zone change here — Utah and Colorado are both MDT in summer. The change happens tomorrow when you cross into Nevada.
Ruby Canyon / Westwater SCENIC
🏔️ I-70 hugs the Colorado River through a narrow red-rock gorge — sheer walls of Entrada sandstone on both sides for several miles. One of the most scenic stretches of the entire drive.

San Rafael Swell SCENIC
🏔️ I-70 cuts through the heart of the San Rafael Swell — jagged red sandstone reefs, dramatically tilted rock layers, and vast open sky. No services for the entire stretch but one of the most dramatic desert drives in the country.
San Rafael Reef Viewpoint OPTIONAL
Pullout near mile marker 91 · Dramatic tilted rock fins visible to the north · Quick stop
