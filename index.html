Morning Departure — Erie to Glenwood Springs
6:00 AM → ~9:15 AM · I-70 West through the Rockies
▼
🏔️ The Scenery: This is one of the most stunning drives in America. I-70 climbs through the Eisenhower/Johnson Tunnel (11,013 ft), then drops through Vail, Avon, and the jaw-dropping Glenwood Canyon — 12 miles of sheer 2,000-ft red rock walls carved by the Colorado River. No stops needed; the drive itself is the reward.
Eisenhower/Johnson Tunnel
Mile 216 · ~1 hr from Erie · Summit of I-70 at 11,013 ft · No stop needed, just admire
Vail Pass & Glenwood Canyon SCENIC
Mile 176–124 · The Colorado River runs alongside the highway through towering canyon walls · Pull-offs available if you want to stretch
💡 If you need a quick dog stop, Glenwood Springs rest area at exit 116 has a grassy area right by the river.

Grand Junction → Utah → St. George
~10:00 AM → ~5:30 PM · Town by town · ~305 miles
▼
🗺️ The Route: I-70 W out of Grand Junction, across the Utah line, through high desert to Green River, then south on I-70 through canyon country to Cove Fort where you pick up I-15 South. Richfield → Beaver → Cedar City → St. George. The last 2 hours on I-15 are easy and fast.
~10:20 AM
Utah State Line PHOTO
~25 mi from Grand Junction · I-70 W · Pull-off at welcome sign · Quick photo if you want it
~11:00 AM
Green River, UT OPTIONAL STOP
Exit 164 · ~60 mi from Grand Junction · Small town, basic services
⚠️ Honest assessment: food options here are very limited — a diner and a gas station. Fine for a gas/stretch/dog break if you need it, but don't count on a real meal. Better to push to Richfield or Cedar City.

🌡️ Heat Note: It's June in the Mojave. Temperatures can be 100°F+ by mid-morning. This is the one stretch to power through without long stops. Your pup will be fine in an air-conditioned car, but don't leave the car off.

Barstow → Los Angeles — The Home Stretch
~11:00 AM → ~12:30–1:30 PM · ~135 miles · I-15 to I-10 / I-210
▼
🛣️ Getting In: From Barstow, I-15 takes you southwest through Cajon Pass and into the San Bernardino Valley. For LA proper, you can take I-10 West (toward downtown), I-210 West (for the Valley), or stay on I-15 south and pick up I-215/60. Check your Tesla nav for current traffic — this stretch varies a lot on Saturday midday.
